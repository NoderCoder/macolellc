How We Work
A repeatable framework. Never a cookie-cutter answer.
Our approach is structured enough to be reliable and flexible enough to fit the reality on the ground.
01

Discover & Define

We listen before we propose. Every engagement begins with deep discovery — understanding your constraints, stakeholders, and the real problem underneath the stated one. We define success criteria upfront so everyone is aligned.

02

Design & Prototype

We architect custom solutions tailored to your environment. No templates, no one-size-fits-all. We prototype where possible, test assumptions early, and iterate before committing resources at scale.

03

Deliver & Implement

Execution is where most engagements fail. We stay hands-on through implementation — managing timelines, coordinating stakeholders, and solving problems in real time. We deliver outcomes, not reports.

04

Measure & Improve

After delivery, we measure what matters. We build feedback mechanisms, track KPIs against the success criteria we defined on day one, and identify opportunities for the next cycle of improvement. Getting better never stops.

About Us
Built in Pittsburgh. Driven by results.

Macole LLC was founded in 2026 by Nicole Turner with a clear conviction: businesses and government agencies deserve partners who solve problems, not partners who sell hours.

As a women-owned small business headquartered in Pittsburgh, we bring entrepreneurial energy and strategic discipline to every engagement. Nicole leads the company as Senior Managing Member, setting the vision and ensuring every client gets direct access to senior leadership — not a junior team.

Our model is simple: understand the problem deeply, design a solution that fits, execute with precision, and keep improving. We operate across consulting, real estate, and procurement because complex problems don't respect industry boundaries — and neither do we.
